Visitor statistics & privacy

We use our Shala Stats service on Cloudflare to understand which parts of the Kalimpong plaza people visit and find useful.

We count page openings, anonymous tab sessions, clicks, approximate scroll depth and active time. We record the page, device category, browser family, referring website and approximate country, region and city. A phone network or VPN may show the wrong city.

For visitor statistics, we do not store raw IP addresses, names, typed searches, form answers, personal shopping-list names, audio or recordings of your screen. We do not request GPS location. The anonymous session ID lasts for the tab; no lasting visitor identifier or tracking cookie is created. Your shopping lists remain on your device.

Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control are respected. You can also stop statistics in this browser. Shopping will keep working.

Message Naveen

If you choose “Message me”, only the message, optional name, optional reply contact and page label you submit are sent to Naveen’s personal Telegram inbox. They are not included in visitor statistics. Delivery references are stored to prevent duplicate sends. A daily salted network hash helps limit abuse; raw IP addresses are not stored. Do not include service numbers, identity documents or sensitive station details. Closing the form clears your text. For deletion of a message, contact Naveen using the same form and its reference.
